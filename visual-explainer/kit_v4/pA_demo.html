Why does a steady worker still need a queue?

  1. Requests arrive in a burst

    Six requests come at the same time. The worker can do one in each tick. Look at the yellow items on the left.

  2. The queue holds the extra

    The worker takes one request. The other five wait in the queue. Nothing is lost.

    —items waiting at the peak
  3. The queue drains

    Each tick, one request finishes and turns green. After the burst, the queue becomes empty again.

  4. Move the slider

    Make the burst bigger. The queue grows, but the worker speed stays the same.

A queue buys time, not speed. If the burst never ends, only a faster worker helps.
